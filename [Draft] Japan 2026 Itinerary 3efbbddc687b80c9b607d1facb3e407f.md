## Hakone

- [Hakone Shrine and the lakeside torii gate](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hakone+Shrine+Hakone+Japan)
- [Lake Ashi](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lake+Ashi+Hakone+Japan) - sightseeing cruise and views of Mount Fuji on clear days
- [Hakone Ropeway](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hakone+Ropeway+Japan) - volcanic views around Owakudani
- [Owakudani](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Owakudani+Hakone+Japan) - steaming volcanic valley and black eggs
- [Hakone Open-Air Museum](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hakone+Open-Air+Museum+Japan) - outdoor sculpture park
- [Gora](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Gora+Hakone+Japan) - gardens and hot springs
