# Stores · floors · what to get

Floors, sibling shops, and souvenirs. The day plan stays in [`area-walks.md`](area-walks.md). Buy a category once. Skip the next shop that sells the same thing.

**Reviews** below are what recent travelers report (r/JapanTravelTips, r/TokyoTravel, r/TrueChefKnives, 2023–2026) plus the tax rules on [Japan Guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/news/tax-free-shopping.html). Coupons and stock change. Check the price tag that day.

### Tax-free on this trip

Through **31 Oct 2026** the tax still comes off in the shop. This trip ends 26 Oct, so that is the rule you use.

- Passport at a tax-free shop. Usual bar is **¥5,000+ before tax, per shop, per day**.
- Some shops take it off at the register. Some send you to a separate counter. Ask before you pay.
- Food, cosmetics, and other consumables stay **sealed until you leave Japan**.
- If you use the automated immigration gates, get a landing stamp after. Some shops still ask to see it.
- From **1 Nov 2026** the refund moves to the airport. That does not apply to this trip.

### Pokémon Center DX · Nihonbashi

~11:00–21:00. 10 min from the hotel. This is the one to buy from.  
**Siblings:** PARCO 6F · Station store · Ikebukuro. Same products. DX has the wider plush wall and more capsule machines, and it does tax-free.

| Floor | What’s there | Get |
|---|---|---|
| 1F | Plush, everyday merch, till | One plush. Pins. |
| 2F | Adult / fashion, premium | A bag or shirt if you want one. Skip the keychain wall |

Pokémon Cafe is a **reservation**, not a shop.

**Reviews:** Travelers who did several centers say DX has the best small-plush selection. Shibuya is packed, the stock is picked through, and the line is for the Mewtwo out front. Location-exclusive Pikachu is uncommon now; [Tokyo Station](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tokyo+Station) and the airports sometimes still have one. If DX is done, PARCO 6F is only worth entering for **Nintendo**.

### Kappabashi (Day 2)

One knife shop, then Ganso. The rest of the street repeats those two.

| Shop | Get | Sibling / skip |
|---|---|---|
| [**Kama-asa**](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kama-asa+Kappabashi) | One santoku or petty. English labels. They will test-cut. Engraving if you have time | Kamata is the price check. Then stop |
| [**Kamata Hakensha**](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kamata+Hakensha+Kappabashi) | Often cheaper on the same kind of knife. English varies | **One till.** Do not buy at both |
| [**Ganso Shokuhin Sample-ya**](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ganso+Shokuhin+Sample-ya+Kappabashi) | Plastic sushi or ramen magnet | Maizuru if this queue is long |
| [**Maizuru**](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Maizuru+food+sample+shop+Kappabashi) | Smaller, cheaper samples | Skip if Ganso is done |

**Reviews (knives):** On r/TrueChefKnives, Kama-asa is the shop people go back to: English labels, staff who explain steel, brands such as Fujitake, Ashi Hamono, Takamura, Misono, Ryusen. Kamata is the other one worth a look and is often the lower price; staff can be fine or short with tourists. Tsubaya has English and a big display, and regulars call the prices high because of the crowds. A Feb 2026 visit found Kama-asa busy but usable, and some of the same knives cheaper in Osaka — so if the price feels high, wait for [Doguyasuji](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Doguyasuji+Osaka) and compare. Buy **stainless** unless someone will wash and dry a carbon blade the same day. **Checked baggage only.** A knife in a cabin bag will be taken at security.

**Reviews (Ganso):** Called the most realistic fake-food shop on the street. The magnet is the item people still use at home. They also sell kits. Do not buy plastic food on Nakamise.

[Kama-asa](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kama-asa+Kappabashi) · [Ganso](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ganso+Shokuhin+Sample-ya+Kappabashi)

### mont-bell · Shibuya flagship

Udagawacho 11-5, across from Tokyu Hands. 10:30–21:00. Tax-free.

| Floor | What’s there | Get |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Tents, sleeping bags, cook kit | Only if you camp |
| 1F | Hats, gloves, umbrellas | Packable umbrella |
| 2F | **Men’s wear** | Light down or a windshell. Try it on |
| 3F | Women’s + kids | Same |
| 4F | Footwear, backpacks, poles | Daypack, merino socks. Trail shoes only if Xebio is skipped |
| 5F | Event space | — |

**Which jacket, from people who own them:** Plasma 1000 is very light and very thin (about 7 denier). Warm for the weight, easy to tear, wrong for rough use. Alpine Light uses a tougher shell (about 20 denier) and is the one to try if you want a jacket you will wear in a city. Japanese sizing runs small. Ask for a Western fit if the shoulders are tight. Staff will tell you the temperature range. One jacket. No tent.

**Backup · Shinjuku South 1F** — same clothes, one floor, Day 2 if Shibuya shopping is skipped. Kyoto Kawaramachi only if both Tokyo stores were skipped.

### MEGA Don Quijote Shibuya (24h)

Follow signs for **食品 / 薬 / 家電**. One visit.

| Zone | Get |
|---|---|
| Snack / 食品 | A Japan-only KitKat or Pocky you did not already buy at a drugstore |
| Drug / 薬 | Only what [Matsumoto Kiyoshi](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Matsumoto+Kiyoshi) did not have |
| Kitchen | Zojirushi mug, chopsticks |
| Electronics | Casio / G-Shock if Bic is skipped. Not cameras |
| Other | Magnets |

**Reviews:** Donki is worth one walk-through because snacks, drugstore items, and electronics are in one building, which makes the ¥5,000 tax-free bar easy. It is not the cheap shop. Skincare, makeup, and KitKats are often ¥100–500 less at [Matsumoto Kiyoshi](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Matsumoto+Kiyoshi), Sundrug, or Welcia, and those lines are shorter. Electronics are often less at Bic or Yodobashi. A large Donki basket sometimes gets an extra coupon on top of the 10% — read the sign at the door; people mention it around a ¥30,000 spend. The tax-free queue itself is long. Do not do a second Donki for the same list. There is a large Daiso in Ginza the same afternoon if you want cheap home goods without the Donki maze.

### Map Camera · Shinjuku (used)

Near the metro government building. About 10:00–20:00. Check [mapcamera.com](https://www.mapcamera.com/) first; the site lags the shop.

**Reviews:** The used shop people name first in Tokyo. Large, graded, staff let you test more than one body, tax-free with a passport. Prices are fair against buying at home, not a giveaway. [Fujiya Camera](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Fujiya+Camera+Nakano) in Nakano is the other one, and you are there on Day 5 — Osaka used shops were sometimes 10–20% less for the same grade, so do not assume Tokyo is the bottom price. Kitamura is usually higher. Akiba is not a camera district. Donki cameras are not graded used stock.

### Ippodo Tea · Kyoto main store

**Get:** one gyokuro or sencha tin, or travel packs. A whisk only if you will use it. 09:00–18:00. Staff will tell you how to brew it.  
**Siblings:** Nishiki Itoh Kyuemon for Uji snacks and regional KitKats. Donki tins are not the same tea.

**Reviews:** The Teramachi main store is the one. The cafe next door (Sayu) has a wait and is separate from buying a tin. Popular matcha sells out. One tin. Nishiki is the snack stop, not a second tea shop.

### Tōji 21st (Day 8, 14:30–15:10, closes 16:00)

**Get:** vintage kimono or haori, about ¥1,000–5,000, cash. Open it in daylight. Check stains, smell, and holes.  
**Siblings:** [2nd STREET](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2nd+STREET) and Ragtag any day, fixed prices. Shimokitazawa for clothes, not for kimono. Teramachi vintage if this market is skipped.

**Reviews:** The 21st is the date. Other days the temple market is not this market. Cash. What looks cheap can be damaged or a costume piece. If you want a known price and a receipt, [2nd STREET](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2nd+STREET) is the ordinary shop.
