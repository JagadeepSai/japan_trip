## 🏙️ Do we have time for another city? — **No, and you shouldn't add one**
You're already hitting **4 bases** (Tokyo, Hakone, Kyoto, Osaka). Adding a real 5th city (Hiroshima ~2 h from Osaka, or Kanazawa) would over-stuff an already-full trip and steal time from the festival + experiences you care about. **The clever move:** your **onsen finish at Arima Onsen sits directly behind Kobe** — so you get a **Kobe taste (beef, harbour) + a top onsen** as your wind-down, no extra leg. Optional single half-day extra: **Nara** (deer + Great Buddha, 45 min from Kyoto/Osaka).

## TOKYO (Days 1–5)
*Base in **Shinjuku** or **Shibuya** — walkable to nightlife, easy to Hakone later.*

### Day 1 — Wed Oct 15 · Land & ease in
- Arrive, drop bags, IC card (Welcome Suica) + eSIM check.
- **Shinjuku** evening: dinner in **Omoide Yokocho** (yakitori alley), then the **Tokyo Metropolitan Gov Building free observatory** (202 m, night skyline) if awake.
- Keep it light — jetlag day.

### Day 2 — Thu Oct 16 · Iconic Tokyo + Ameyoko + nightlife
| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| AM | **Asakusa** — Senso-ji, Kaminarimon, Nakamise street |
| Late AM | **Ueno** — Ueno Park + **Ameyoko Market** (grazing, cheap goods) |
| PM | **teamLab Planets** (Toyosu, barefoot digital art — *book ahead*) |
| Dusk | **Shibuya** — Scramble Crossing, Hachiko, **Shibuya Sky** sunset (*book the sunset slot*) |
| Night | Shibuya nightlife — izakaya + a bar/club |

### Day 3 — Fri Oct 17 · Akihabara + Nakano + club night
| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| AM–PM | **Akihabara**: **mega arcades** (GiGO, Taito Station; **Super Potato** retro gaming), **maid cafe** (@Home cafe), **purikura** booths, gachapon halls, electronics/anime shopping (Yodobashi, Mandarake, Radio Kaikan) |
| Late PM | **Nakano Broadway** (retro/otaku collector's maze — Mandarake HQ) |
| Night | Big night out — **Shibuya (Womb) / Shin-Kiba ageHa (EDM) / Roppongi**; or all-night **karaoke** |
> *Optional swap:* Oct 17 is also the **Kawagoe Festival** (30 min out) — if a Little-Edo float festival appeals more than an Akihabara full day, do Kawagoe in the afternoon/evening and compress Akihabara into a morning.

### Day 4 — Sat Oct 18 · Okutama adventure day
- **Okutama / Mitake (Ome area), ~1.5–2 h from Shinjuku (JR Chuo/Ome line).**
- **White-water rafting or canyoning** on the Tama River — the group adrenaline hit; half-day trips, wetsuits provided.
- ⚠️ **Verify October operation:** this is the **tail of the season** (some operators run to late Oct/early Nov, water is cold — wetsuit weather). **Book ahead.** If closed, swap in **Mt Takao** (easy hike + cablecar + base onsen) as the nature day.
- Evening: back to Tokyo, relaxed izakaya.

### Day 5 — Sun Oct 19 · Markets, Harajuku & Golden Gai by day
| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| AM | **Flea market** — **Oedo Antique Market** at Tokyo International Forum (runs **1st & 3rd Sundays**; Oct 19 is the 3rd Sunday — *verify 2026 dates*). Backup: Nogi-jinja / Shinjuku flea markets |
| Midday | **Harajuku** — Takeshita St, Meiji Shrine, Omotesando/Cat Street; **Yoyogi Park** Sunday buskers |
| Afternoon | **Golden Gai by day** — the 200-bar lantern maze is quiet and photogenic in daylight; a few daytime cafés/bars open ~noon (most open evening) |
| Night | Last Tokyo night — your pick (a Golden Gai bar now that it's alive, or a final club) |

> **Tokyo shopping covered:** Akihabara (Day 3), Nakano Broadway (Day 3), Harajuku (Day 5), Ameyoko (Day 2), flea market (Day 5). Add **Don Quijote** (24 h, anywhere) for souvenirs and **Kappabashi** (kitchen knives) if keen.

## HAKONE (Days 6–7) · foliage + onsen + Fuji
*Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku (~85 min). Buy the **Hakone Freepass** (~¥7,100) — covers the whole loop.*

### Day 6 — Mon Oct 20 · Hakone loop + onsen night
- The circuit: **Hakone Open-Air Museum** → **Owakudani** volcanic valley (ropeway, black eggs) → **Lake Ashi** pirate-ship cruise (torii + Fuji views).
- **Onsen ryokan overnight** — kaiseki dinner, private/public baths. *(Autumn = crisp air and better **Fuji visibility** than summer; foliage is starting at Hakone's elevation.)*
- *Book the ryokan 12–16 weeks ahead for October.*

### Day 7 — Tue Oct 21 · Hakone AM → Kyoto
- Morning: **Old Tokaido cedar-avenue walk** by Lake Ashi, or one more Fuji viewpoint / open-air onsen.
- Midday: Hakone-Yumoto → **Odawara/Mishima → shinkansen to Kyoto** (~2–2.5 h).
- Evening: check in near **Gion/Kawaramachi**; dinner + drinks on **Pontocho/Kiyamachi**.

## KYOTO (Days 8–10) · the festival + your Kyoto list
*Base near **Gion / Kawaramachi** (riverside, walkable, nightlife).*

### Day 8 — **Wed Oct 22 · FESTIVAL DAY** (the centerpiece)
| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| Morning | **Samurai & Ninja Museum + experience** (central Kyoto/Nakagyo) — sword/ninja-star training, armour, photos (~¥3,000–5,000; ~1.5 h) |
| ~12:00 | **Jidai Matsuri** — the "Festival of the Ages" procession leaves the **Imperial Palace ~12:00** → **Heian Shrine** (~14:30). Grab a kerb spot on Oike-dori early, or a paid grandstand seat |
| Late afternoon | Head **north to Kurama early** (Eizan line from Demachiyanagi) — it jams badly |
| Night | **Kurama Fire Festival (Himatsuri)** — giant flaming torches up to the shrine, sparks and roar. Be ready for a **very crowded, very late return** |
> *This is the highlight of the trip — protect the energy for the night. Keep the morning light.*

### Day 9 — Thu Oct 23 · Fushimi Inari + kimono + Gion + Nishiki + Gion Corner
| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| **Dawn (before 7:30)** | **Fushimi Inari Taisha — full mountain loop hike** (the 10,000 torii; the whole Mt Inari loop is ~2–3 h and blissfully empty early) |
| Late AM | Back downtown → **kimono / yukata rental** for the day (~¥5,000–6,000) |
| Midday | **Nishiki Market** — "Kyoto's kitchen," grazing + **walk across the city** through the old lanes |
| Afternoon | **Gion + Higashiyama** in your kimono (Ninenzaka/Sannenzaka, Yasaka Shrine) — mind the geiko-photo ban in private alleys (¥10,000 fine) |
| Evening (~18:00) | **Gion Corner show** — a sampler of maiko dance, tea ceremony, kyogen, koto (*verify the current schedule/season; book ahead*) |

### Day 10 — Fri Oct 24 · Arashiyama: Tenryu-ji + countryside cycling
| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| AM | **Arashiyama** — **Tenryu-ji** (zen temple + garden) + the **bamboo grove** early |
| Midday | **Countryside cycling** — rent bikes near Arashiyama and ride the **Sagano rural lanes** (rice fields, Adashino, Okochi Sanso, Saga-Toriimoto old street). Relaxed, green, un-touristy. *(Alt: the Ohara valley north of Kyoto for deeper countryside.)* |
| Afternoon | Katsura riverside / monkey park, or a Fushimi **sake-brewery tasting** |
| Evening | Last Kyoto night — Pontocho or a hidden-lane izakaya |

> *Optional Nara half-day:* if you'd rather one more place than a slow Arashiyama, swap the afternoon for **Nara** (45 min: bowing deer + Todai-ji Great Buddha).

## OSAKA + ONSEN FINISH (Days 11–12)

### Day 11 — Sat Oct 25 · Osaka energy → Arima Onsen
| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| AM | Kyoto → **Osaka** (~15 min). Drop bags. **Kuromon Market** grazing |
| Day | **Dotonbori** (Glico sign, street food — takoyaki/okonomiyaki/kushikatsu), **America-mura** (youth/streetwear), **Shinsekai/Tsutenkaku**, or **Umeda Sky** / **Abeno Harukas** view. *(USJ + Super Nintendo World is a full alternative day if you'd swap it in — book Express ahead.)* |
| Early eve | Dotonbori neon at dusk, then train to **Arima Onsen** (~1–1.5 h via Kobe/Sannomiya) |
| Night | **Arima Onsen ryokan** — Japan's oldest hot-spring town; gold (*kinsen*) & silver (*ginsen*) baths. The relaxing finish you wanted |

### Day 12 — Sun Oct 26 · Soak, Kobe bite, fly home
- Morning: final onsen soak; optional **Mt Rokko ropeway** or drop into **Kobe** (Nankinmachi Chinatown, harbour, a Kobe-beef lunch) — Arima sits right behind Kobe.
- Then to **KIX** (Arima → Kobe/Osaka → Nankai/Haruka; allow ~2 h + buffer) → fly home.

## ✂️ If you only have 10 days (Oct 15–24)
Cut in this order, least painful first:
1. **Drop the Arima onsen finish** → your onsen night becomes **Hakone (Day 6)**; end in Osaka and fly out Day 11. *(You still get an onsen, just not at the very end.)*
2. **Trim one Tokyo day** — merge Nakano Broadway into the Akihabara day and drop the slowest half-day.
3. **Make Osaka an evening only** — a Dotonbori night as a day-trip from Kyoto (15 min), fly out next morning.
Keep untouched: the **Oct 22 festival day**, **Fushimi Inari loop**, and the **Hakone onsen night**.
