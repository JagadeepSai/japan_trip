# Japan 2026 · Flexible itinerary

Area days from the hotel door. **Food is never a route stop.** A lunch window and a dinner window sit where you already are; pick from the shortlist, or a combini / depachika. **Shopping is optional** — every afternoon has a See track when the shops are done.

**How to use a day**

1. **Anchors** — timed ticket, train, or hotel cutoff. Don’t move these.
2. **Core walk** — the area spine (sights). This is the Maps route.
3. **Lunch / dinner window** — 60–75 min, nearby options only. Not on the map.
4. **Split** — Shop track (shop-to-shop) **or** See track. Same area. Floors: [`stores.md`](stores.md).

Nothing comes off a day unless you say so. Each day ends with **Also in this area** — things from earlier lists that are not on the clock. Say which ones to slot.

🌱 vegetarian/vegan. **🥩 beef** — eat it, just marked so you can pick or skip in a mixed group. 24h backup: **Matsuya / Yoshinoya / Sukiya** (default bowl is 🥩 gyudon; pork/fish also on the menu) · 7-Eleven / FamilyMart / Lawson · station **depachika**.

### End of day · Yoshiwara · 19:30–23:00 · 18+ only

[**Yoshiwara**](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Yoshiwara+Omon+Taito) (Senzoku, Taito) is the licensed soapland district. About 20 min by taxi from the hotel, or Hibiya line to Minowa and a short taxi. Most shops stop taking guests around midnight. Hakone is 07:30, so leave in time to be in the hotel by **00:30**.

This is a licensed bathhouse with a sexual service. Adults only. Bring your passport.

**How to do it without getting cheated**

- Pick the shop yourself. A licensed shop has an address and a price on the board or its own site. It does not send someone to recruit you on the street.
- Anyone who walks up offering a “cheap girl,” a “muscle girl,” or a shop “just around the corner” is a tout. Taito ward bans that kind of street soliciting. Say no and keep walking.
- Do this in Yoshiwara. Do not do it in Kabukicho. Shinjuku has very few soaplands, and the street trade there is the scam.
- Before you pay, confirm three things: they accept a foreign guest, the total in yen, and the number of minutes. Some shops add a foreigner fee or refuse. If they refuse, leave.
- Cash. The number on the board that day is the price. Reported ranges are roughly ¥20,000–¥60,000 for 60–120 minutes. High-end shops cost more. Cheap shops are where the photo on the panel does not match the person.
- No photos of staff. Condoms are part of the service.
- Call the number on the shop’s own site that afternoon if you want a specific person. Do not trust a stranger to book it for you.
